Dog Fight

The ultimate couch party game. Up to 8 players, one screen, frantic air combat — duel head to head, team up, or hold off endless waves together. Online and LAN too.

Eight friends. One screen. No survivors.

Dog Fight is a couch party game about tiny planes and terrible decisions. Grab up to eight controllers, pick a side, and turn the living room into a warzone. Rounds are short, the controls take about four seconds to learn, and the loser always wants one more go.

Play together, on one screen

  • Up to 8 players locally — versus, in teams, or co-operatively
  • Bots fill the empty seats, so a two-player night still feels like a full lobby — or practise against them solo
  • Pickups scattered through every arena swing a dogfight in a second: grab first, aim later
  • Online and LAN as well, for when the couch has to be a metaphor

Four modes

  • Invasion — team up and hold off endless waves of ever-amassing enemies
  • Team Free-for-All — pick sides and fight it out
  • Tag Team — one pilot up at a time, tag out when you're in trouble
  • Rabbit — one player is the rabbit, everyone else wants what they've got
